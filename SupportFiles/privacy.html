Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

Mac AI Helper ("the App") is developed and maintained by Castloud. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App handles and how it is treated.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We have no backend, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • API Keys — Your AI provider API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini) are stored securely in the macOS Keychain on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Settings — Your prompt templates, keyboard shortcuts, and preferences are saved locally in the Application Support directory.

Data Sent to Third Parties

When you use the App to analyze text or screenshots, the content you submit is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you have configured (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google). This communication happens between your Mac and the provider's API — it does not pass through any Castloud servers.

Please review the privacy policies of the AI providers you use:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Screen Recording

The screenshot feature uses macOS ScreenCaptureKit to capture a region of your screen. The captured image is processed locally and sent only to the AI provider you have configured. Screenshots are not stored permanently and are discarded after the analysis is complete.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional tip jar purchases through Apple's StoreKit. These transactions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive any personal or payment information from these transactions.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@castloud.com.
